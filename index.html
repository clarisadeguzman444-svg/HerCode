A Little More of Her

Hi, I'm Clarisa Mae De Guzman, an 18-year-old girl who somehow found herself pursuing Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at University of Eastern Pangasinan, a path she never quite imagined herself taking, and also accidentally became a Block Mayor of her beloved 1G pips. Neither was exactly part of the original plan, but I guess life has its own funny ways of leading us somewhere unexpected.


I'm interested in books, science, psychology, traveling, being there for people, and a hundred other things that make this little world interesting. I love learning about how things work, understanding why people think and feel the way they do, discovering places beyond the familiar, and being someone others can turn to when they need a little understanding.


Truthfully, I'm still figuring out what to do with my life, where I want to go, and who I want to become. I don't have everything figured out just yet, and I think that's okay. For now, I'm learning to be content with where I am, appreciate the people walking beside me, and find happiness in the little moments that make this chapter of my life worth remembering. After all, I may not know exactly where life is taking me, but I'm grateful for where I am and the people I get to share this journey with. ♡
